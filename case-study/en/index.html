The problem

Card fraud is adversarial, extremely imbalanced (~1% of transactions) and unforgiving: every blocked purchase needs a justification, every missed fraud costs money. A model that predicts "legitimate" for everything scores 99% accuracy and catches zero fraud — which is why this project treats explainability and calibration as first-class requirements, not afterthoughts.

The answer is a hybrid: rules for transparency, gradient boosting for pattern discovery, graphs and embeddings for network-level signals, and infrastructure that keeps all of it honest over time.
