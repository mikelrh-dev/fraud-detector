Chapter 01

Architecture End-to-End

From raw transaction to explained verdict: three layers, async workers, one auditable score.

Every transaction flows through three independent scoring layers. Their outputs are combined into a single 0–100 risk score:

risk_score = 0.60 × rule_score + 0.25 × ml_score + 0.15 × context_score
// weights configurable via environment, no retraining needed to tune

The final score is compared against a dynamic threshold (Chapter 5) to classify each transaction as legitimate, review, or fraud. Flagged transactions are persisted and pushed to async workers that enrich them with LLM reports, SHAP attributions and merchant embedding analysis — without ever blocking the API response path.
